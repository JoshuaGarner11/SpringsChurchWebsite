Billy Coker is the Executive Pastor of Springs Church and holds a Masters of Divinity degree from Liberty University. He worked as a business strategist in the financial services industry until joining the staff in 2022. He is blessed to serve the staff, working together to bring Springs Church's vision to life through their ministries. Billy also oversees church operations to ensure good stewardship. He and his wife, Danielle, have three children and been a part of Springs Church since 2010.
