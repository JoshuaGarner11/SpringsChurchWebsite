Gary has worked in ministry since college with Athletes in Action (Cru), Focus on the Family, Wild at Heart, and founded The Noble Heart Ministry, speaking around the world on personal calling. He is the author of the book, It’s Your Call. He leads Springs Men and the Security Team. Gary and his wife, Leigh, have lived in Colorado Springs for 40 years. They have 4 children and 8 grandchildren.
