michael-petillo

Michael Petillo is the Lead Pastor of Springs Church. He grew up pursuing a career in photography until God got a hold of his heart and he enrolled in Bible college. After graduating, he served overseas and worked at World Challenge before accepting the role as Youth Pastor of Springs Church for six years. In 2017, Michael was invited to become Lead Pastor. He and his wife, Beth, who leads Springs Women, have three children and have been a part of Springs Church since the beginning.
