Andy has been involved in youth ministry, education, and missions for 40 years. He has his Masters in Religious Curriculum and Instruction (CCU), Youth Ministries (Fuller), and is a Fellow at the Colson Center for Biblical Worldview. Andy is on the Missions Board, and is involved with membership, community engagement, and the youth ministry. He has served as an Elder since 2014. Andy, and his wife Angie, have been involved with Springs Church since its founding in 2009. They have four grown children, all of whom were raised at Springs Church.
