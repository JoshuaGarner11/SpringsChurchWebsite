Tithing

When you tithe, you’re trusting God with your finances.

Tithing is a biblical principle. God calls us to give at the very least, the first 10% of our income back to him. In fact, it’s the one area in the Bible where God tells you to test him. Offerings and alms are any additional giving beyond basic tithing. We know God will provide abundantly for you and your family when you put Him first in your finances.

“Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, that there may be food in my house. And thereby put me to the test, says the Lord of hosts, if I will not open the windows of heaven for you and pour down for you a blessing until there is no more need.”

Malachi 3:10 (ESV)

Giving Questions

Still wondering about something? Email info@springs.church.

Is giving online secure?

Yes. Your gift is processed by Pushpay, a payment company built for churches. Springs Church never sees or stores your card or bank details.

Will I get a giving statement?

Pushpay emails a receipt for every gift, and your full giving history is in your Pushpay account.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Gifts to The Springs Church Inc. are tax-deductible as allowed by law.

Can I give to a specific missionary?

Yes. Choose Missions above, then pick a partner under “Send it to,” or tap any partner in Our Missions Partners.
