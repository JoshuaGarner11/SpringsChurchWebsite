Host an Event?

Ministries, members and outside groups can request space at Springs for conferences, concerts, graduations, weddings and more.

Request an Event events@springs.church
Ask Early
Send requests at least 30 days ahead, and earlier for weddings and large events. We reply within two weeks.
Have the Details Ready
Date and times, how many people are coming, which rooms you’d like, setup and teardown time and catering.
Extras Aren’t Guaranteed
You can ask for tables, chairs, decor, sound, video or livestreaming. We’ll confirm what’s available.
Springs Ministry Events
Tell us if you need promotion or a registration form.
Weddings & Funerals
Weddings are for members and regular attenders, with premarital preparation. Memorial services and receptions are for members. Our Care Team helps plan both.
Facility Guidelines

Booking

  • A request isn’t a reservation. Your date is held once we confirm it.
  • Church services and Springs ministry events come first, so Sunday mornings and Wednesday nights are usually unavailable.
  • Outside groups may be asked to cover costs like custodial, security or tech staff. We’ll share any costs before we confirm.
  • Outside organizations may need to provide a certificate of liability insurance.

On Campus

  • Events should fit what we believe and our mission.
  • No alcohol, tobacco, vaping or illegal drugs anywhere on campus.
  • Your group supervises any kids who come. Springs Kids rooms aren’t included unless we arrange it.
  • Food and drinks stay in approved spaces. Caterers must be licensed, and kitchen use is by arrangement.
  • No tape, nails, glitter or confetti on walls and floors. Open flames need approval.
  • Sound, lights, video and livestreaming are run by Springs tech staff or approved volunteers.

Wrapping Up

  • Plan setup and teardown inside your reserved time.
  • Leave rooms the way you found them, with trash out and tables and chairs back in place.
  • Let us know right away if your plans or numbers change, or if you need to cancel.
