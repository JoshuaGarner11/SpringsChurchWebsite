Your First Sunday

  1. Before 10AM

    Arriving

    Our Parking Team will point you to a spot and the right door. Inside, someone from our Connect Team will say hello, help you find a seat, and answer any questions.

  2. Bringing kids?

    Kids Check-In

    Stop at the Kids check-in counter when you arrive. A friendly volunteer will get your family set up in a couple of minutes.

    Your child gets a name tag and you get a matching pickup tag. Only the grown-up holding that tag can pick them up.

    More for parents

  3. Before 10AM

    Coffee

    Grab a cup at the coffee bar. Want to talk with someone? Let’s Connect Over Coffee before service.

  4. 10AM

    Worship and Teaching

    Spirit-filled worship and Biblical preaching. Kids are in their own rooms during service.

  5. After service

    Prayer and Pickup

    Our Prayer Team is at the altar after every service if you’d like someone to pray with you.

    Bring your tag back to your child’s room to pick them up.
