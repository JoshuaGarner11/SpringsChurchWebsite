Before You Walk In

Sundays, before the 10 AM service

For a lot of people, the first person they meet at Springs is in the parking lot or at the door. A warm hello and a good cup of coffee go a long way.

  • Parking Team

    Greet people as they pull in and help them find a spot and the right door.

    Sundays

  • Connect Team

    Welcome people at the doors, help them find their way and a seat, and answer their questions.

    Sundays

  • Coffee Bar

    Serve fresh coffee, drinks and snacks to everyone who walks in.

    Sundays

How Joining Works

Thank you for your interest in volunteering at Springs Church. Here’s what to expect before you start.

One Team at a Time
Each application is for one team. Interested in two? Send an application for each.
What It Asks
Your contact information and mailing address, a little about your faith and how long you’ve been at Springs, and whether you’re a member.
Our Statement of Faith
Everyone who serves on a team agrees to our Statement of Faith. Read it before you start.
Background Checks
Kids and Youth teams and the Security Team need a background check before you serve.
What Happens Next
We’ll reach out to you shortly. We’re looking forward to serving with you.
Pick a team first

Not sure where you fit? Ask us. We’ll help you find a place.
