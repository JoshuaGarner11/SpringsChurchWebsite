What to Expect

An opportunity to meet new friends, draw closer to Jesus and be encouraged to live more passionately for God.

  • A Warm Welcome

    Friendly faces ready to greet you and help you feel at home.

  • Biblical Teaching

    Christ-centered messages you can apply to daily life.

  • Engaging Worship

    Spirit-filled praise that draws your heart closer to God.

  • Dynamic Prayer

    Talking to God by aligning our faith with his promises.

  • Authentic Community

    Connect with others and build meaningful relationships.

  • Safe Kids Ministry

    A fun and secure place for children to learn about Jesus.
