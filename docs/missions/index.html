      Give to Missions

      When you give to missions, you are helping share the love of Jesus beyond the walls of our church. Your generosity supports missionaries, church plants, and outreach efforts that bring hope to communities locally and around the world. Every gift makes a difference, advancing the gospel and meeting real needs as we join together in God’s mission.

      By giving through Springs Church, 100% of your donation goes directly to the individual missionary and not toward administrative costs.

      Since our first meeting in 2009, this church has given more than $5 million to reach people around the world.
